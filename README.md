# KieSec

**Offensive Security · Systems Research · Digital Forensics**

A growing collection of security investigations, lab write-ups, and technical notes by [Kiemar Lopes](https://github.com/Kiemar).

This repository documents my exploration of how systems work, where their security assumptions break down, and what the evidence reveals. The aim is to connect practical testing with clear explanations, defensible conclusions, and useful remediation.

## Areas of Study

My interests span web and API security, Active Directory, malware analysis, reverse engineering, hardware and embedded systems, AWS and Azure, OSINT, digital forensics, and AI security—including the behavior and reliability of multi-agent systems.

## How I Approach a Project

For each substantial investigation, I aim to explain:

- **The question:** What am I investigating, and what is the scope?
- **The reasoning:** Why did I choose these tests and methods?
- **The evidence:** What did I observe, and what does it establish?
- **The limitations:** What remains uncertain or untested?
- **The response:** What could prevent, detect, or remediate the issue?
- **The validation:** How did I check whether the proposed improvement worked?

Write-ups distinguish independent work from guided exercises and acknowledge sources and assistance.
